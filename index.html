◈ DEVELOPER'S DISPATCH & TECHNICAL ARCHIVE

Kshitij Adakane

Technical Head · Website Developer · TRIGUNA Event Coordinator · Dept. of IIoT

⚡ "To every contestant who dared to step into the arena, every coordinator who stood guard at the consoles, and our faculty mentor who believed in us — a hearty thank you from the developer!"

Building the official digital battleground and portal for XTASY 4.0 has been one of the most fulfilling journeys of my college life. From engineering the Squid Game-inspired interactive SVG canvas and real-time audio synchronization to crafting live telemetry boot sequences, countdown timers, and seamless registration pipelines — every single line of code was written with passion and pride for our department.

"Lines of code can build an arena, but it was the electric energy of 370+ contestants, the dedication of our core team, and the trust of our mentors that gave XTASY 4.0 its beating heart."

Serving as TRIGUNA Event Coordinator while concurrently managing the technological ecosystem as Technical Head & Website Developer demanded relentless hustle and precision. A heartfelt thanks to our Faculty Mentor Prof. Rajeshwari Shinde for her constant guidance and trust, our respected faculty guides Sushant Sir & Shyam Sir for directly helping and backing us with essential tools, equipment, and crucial directions at just the right time, our Coordinators Vidhi Ukey & Roopam Zade for leading the frontline, Promotion Head Abhaya Deogade for driving campus outreach, Registration Head Charul Sawajkar for managing registrations, Documentation Head Khushbu Navghare for meticulous record keeping, Finance Head Shreyash Paunikar, Decoration Head Rahi Ghode, our on-ground execution pillar and official 'Thekedar' Vaibhav Ghode, Media Head Tushar Agashe for stellar video production, and every student volunteer whose grit made this benchmark event possible.

Seeing all of you navigate this portal, register with excitement, and compete with fierce spirit made every sleepless night completely worthwhile. Thank you for making XTASY 4.0 unforgettable!
